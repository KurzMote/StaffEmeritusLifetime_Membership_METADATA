Membership Benefits

1. Mote Aquarium Membership and Tickets

Mote employees receive a Party of Six Membership, renewed annually in September. Your membership is good for 6 in total to come to Mote Aquarium.  The Mote staff member must be the person to request guest tickets in advance. To book your tickets ahead of time, please email guestservices@mote.org or call ext. 416. Details about Mote memberships may be found at mote.org/support/membership.

Mote employees are also allowed up to 4 free guest passes per year. These are physical, undated tickets you can give out to your guests when you are not here to accompany them with your membership. Please see Jessica Rhodes or Lynsay Callies in Guest Services for these passes.

2. Mote Aquarium belongs to the Florida Attractions Association (FAA)

Mote Aquarium is a Premier Attraction on the Florida Attractions Association list. This entitles Mote employees with consistent hours to visit other Florida Attractions in the Association with a completed ‘AttractionShare Letter’ that you may obtain from Veronica Gilbert. The AttractionShare Letter is only available to Mote Staff members and the benefit includes 1 free ticket for the Mote employee and free or % discount tickets for up to 3 guests accompanying the employee to the attraction.

Some of the local attractions on the FAA list include:

  • Sarasota Jungle Gardens: Free admission for yourself & 3 guests
  • Selby Gardens: Free admission for yourself & 3 guests
  • Zoo Tampa: Free admission for yourself & 3 guests
  • Busch Gardens, Tampa: Free admission for yourself & 3 guests at 50% off admission
  • iFly Tampa Indoor Skydiving: Free admission for yourself & 3 guests
  • + Many more!

Check out attractionshare.com for the list of participating attractions and current descriptions. Please provide notice at least 7 days before by email to Jessica Rhodes, Guest Services Manager or Lynsay Callies, Assistant Guest Services Manager at: jrhodes@mote.org or lcallies@mote.org. You must have your AttractionShare Letter to receive the discount at another attraction and you may only visit a particular Attraction once in a given year. Once a letter is written and recorded, it counts as your visit to that attraction. There are maximum limits for White Letters in a given month or year. Please request 7 days in advance and include the following information in your request:

  1. The attraction you wish to visit.
  2. The date you wish to visit the attraction.
  3. How many guests will accompany you? (Up to 3)
  4. Your full legal name.

3. AZA Reciprocal Zoos & Aquariums

Your membership also provides you with reciprocal member benefits at AZA Attractions all over the country. Typically, reciprocating zoos / aquariums offer 50% off admission tickets for you and the amount of guests listed on your membership card. We recommend calling the attraction you plan to visit in advance to check their policies. For the list of participating zoos and aquariums go to mote.org/support/membership and scroll down to download the reciprocal list.

4. Additional Benefits

  • Ringling Museum allows free admission (for you plus 3) year round, by showing your Mote employee ID.
  • The Salvador Dali Museum in St. Pete allows free admission for the month of June (for you and 1 additional adult) by showing your Mote Membership Card.
  • Peace River Botanical Gardens in Punta Gorda allows free admission in the months of July & August for you and your guests by showing your Mote Membership Card.
  • The Edison & Ford Winter Estates in Fort Myers allows free admission for the month of September for you and your guests with your Mote Membership Card.
  • The Sarasota Art Museum allows free admission for the month of October for you and your guests by showing your Mote Membership Card.
  • Bishop Museum allows free admission for the month of November for you and your guest by showing your Mote Membership Card.
  • The Great Explorations Children’s Museum in St. Pete allows free admission for the month of December for you and your guests with your Mote Membership Card.
  • The IMAG Museum of History & Science in Fort Myers allows free admission for the month of January for you and your guests by showing your Mote Membership Card.
  • The Florida Holocaust Museum in St. Pete allows free admission in the months of February, May, August & November for you and your guests by showing your Mote Membership Card.

We always recommended calling the reciprocal insitutuion or checking with the membership office before your visit to verify any changes or stipulations.
